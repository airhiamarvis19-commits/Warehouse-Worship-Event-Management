# 02 — Project Brief

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** Warehouse Worship – The Gathering 2026  
**Project Type:** Large-scale live worship and music event  
**Location:** Manchester Central Convention Complex, Manchester  
**Event Date:** 8 August 2026  
**Doors:** 4:30 PM  
**Event Time:** 5:00 PM – 11:00 PM  
**Organiser:** Warehouse Worship

This project brief establishes an independent project-management framework for analysing and planning a large-scale worship and live music event.

The framework considers the planning, coordination, delivery and evaluation of the event across multiple project workstreams.

---

## 2. Project Aim

The primary aim of the project is:

> **To plan and deliver a safe, well-coordinated and engaging large-scale worship event that provides a positive attendee experience while meeting programme, operational and production objectives.**

---

## 3. Project Objectives

The project has the following objectives:

### Objective 1 — Attendee Experience

Provide a structured attendee journey covering:

**Arrival → Entry → Event → Facilities → Departure**

with clear information and appropriate support throughout.

### Objective 2 — Safety

Establish appropriate crowd-management, security, emergency and risk-control measures to support safe event delivery.

### Objective 3 — Programme Delivery

Coordinate performers, stage management and production requirements to support delivery within the planned event window.

### Objective 4 — Stakeholder Coordination

Maintain clear communication between the organiser, venue, performers, production teams, staff, suppliers and attendees.

### Objective 5 — Operational Efficiency

Identify and reduce avoidable delays and congestion, particularly during peak arrival and entry periods.

### Objective 6 — Post-Event Evaluation

Establish measurable performance indicators and evaluate the attendee experience to identify lessons for future events.

## 6. Key Constraints

### Time

The event operates within a fixed event date and venue operating window.

### Venue

Planning must account for the physical layout, access arrangements, facilities and operational requirements of the selected venue.

### Budget

Available resources must be allocated across venue, production, staffing, security, marketing, transport and other project requirements.

### Safety

Crowd movement, security, emergency access and incident response must be considered throughout the project.

### Stakeholders

The project involves multiple stakeholders with different requirements, levels of influence and information needs.

### Attendance

A large audience creates additional requirements for ticketing, entry, crowd management, facilities and transport.

---

## 7. Project Assumptions

For the purpose of this planning model, the following assumptions are made:

- Appropriate venue capacity is available.
- Necessary permissions and approvals are obtained.
- Artists and suppliers are confirmed in advance.
- Adequate event staff and security personnel are available.
- Production equipment can be delivered and tested before doors open.
- Emergency procedures are agreed with relevant stakeholders.
- Attendees receive appropriate pre-event information.
- Transport arrangements can be coordinated in advance.

These are **planning assumptions for the case study** and are not claims about the organiser's internal arrangements.

---

## 8. Success Criteria

The project will use the following success criteria:

- Safe event delivery
- Effective crowd management
- Programme delivered substantially as planned
- Clear attendee communication
- Efficient entry and exit processes
- Effective stakeholder coordination
- Minimal significant operational incidents
- Positive attendee feedback
- Achievement of defined project objectives
- Delivery within the approved illustrative budget

## 10. Project Lifecycle

The project follows the following lifecycle:

### Initiation

Define the project, objectives, scope and stakeholders.

↓

### Planning

Develop the schedule, budget, risk register, stakeholder plan and operational requirements.

↓

### Preparation

Coordinate venue, suppliers, artists, production, staffing, marketing and transport.

↓

### Event Delivery

Execute the event-day operational plan and monitor performance.

↓

### Monitoring & Control

Monitor risks, schedule, budget, safety, stakeholder requirements and attendee experience.

↓

### Closeout

Complete venue handover, supplier closeout, financial review and post-event evaluation.

↓

### Lessons Learned

Identify improvements and recommendations for future events.
