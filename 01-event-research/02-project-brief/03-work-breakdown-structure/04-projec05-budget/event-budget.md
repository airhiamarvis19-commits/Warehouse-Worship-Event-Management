# Illustrative Event Budget – Warehouse Worship: The Gathering 2026

## 1. Budget Overview

This budget provides an illustrative financial model for a large-scale worship event. The figures are planning assumptions created for this portfolio case study and do not represent the actual budget of Warehouse Worship.

The budget covers venue costs, production, artists, staffing, marketing, safety, transport, hospitality and other operational requirements.

## 3. Budget Allocation

The largest areas of expenditure are production and technical delivery, artist costs and venue provision.

Production costs include stage infrastructure, audio, lighting, screens and associated technical requirements. Artist costs represent an illustrative allocation for performers and programme contributors.

Marketing expenditure supports awareness, audience engagement and ticket conversion, while security, staffing, medical provision and insurance support safe event delivery.

---

## 4. Contingency

A contingency allowance of 10% has been included to provide financial flexibility for unforeseen costs.

Potential uses of contingency could include:

- Additional technical requirements
- Supplier cost increases
- Additional security or staffing
- Transport changes
- Emergency repairs or replacement equipment
- Additional event-day operational requirements

The contingency should only be released through an appropriate approval process.

---

## 5. Budget Control

The project manager would monitor expenditure throughout the project lifecycle.

A basic budget-control process would include:

1. Establish the approved baseline budget.
2. Assign budget ownership to relevant workstreams.
3. Record committed and actual expenditure.
4. Compare actual expenditure against the approved budget.
5. Identify variances.
6. Investigate significant variances.
7. Approve changes through change control.
8. Maintain a current forecast to completion.

---

## 6. Example Budget Controls

| Control | Purpose |

| Purchase orders/contracts | Records financial commitments |
| Monthly budget review | Identifies emerging variances |
| Contingency approval | Controls use of reserve funds |
| Final reconciliation | Confirms final project expenditure |

---

## 7. Key Financial Risks

Potential financial risks include:

- Supplier price increases
- Additional production requirements
- Higher-than-expected staffing requirements
- Security and crowd-management costs increasing
- Transport cost changes
- Cancellation or replacement costs associated with programme changes
- Unexpected venue or technical requirements
- Lower-than-expected ticket revenue

These risks would be monitored through the project's risk register and financial reporting process.

---

## 8. Budget Assumption

All financial figures in this document are **illustrative estimates developed for portfolio purposes**.

They should not be interpreted as the actual financial information, supplier quotations or expenditure of Warehouse Worship.

The budget demonstrates how I would structure and control costs when planning a large-scale event.
