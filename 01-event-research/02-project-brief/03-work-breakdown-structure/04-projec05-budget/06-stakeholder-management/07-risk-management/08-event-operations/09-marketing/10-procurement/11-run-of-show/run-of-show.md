# Event-Day Run of Show – Warehouse Worship: The Gathering 2026

## 1. Purpose

This run of show provides an illustrative event-day framework for coordinating the main operational, programme and production activities.

It combines confirmed public event timings and published programme information with proposed operational assumptions.

It is not presented as the actual internal run of show used by Warehouse Worship.

## 6. Programme Change Management

If a programme element changes, the following process should be used:

**Change identified → Programme Lead notified → Event Lead assesses impact → Production / Operations informed → Approved communication prepared → Staff briefed → Attendees informed where required**

The objective is to ensure that programme changes do not create unnecessary confusion for attendees or operational teams.

---

## 7. Production Coordination

Production should monitor:

- Stage readiness
- Audio systems
- Lighting
- Screens
- Video
- Microphones
- Artist changeovers
- Recording systems
- Technical communications

Technical issues should be escalated through the Production Lead.

Where practical, backup equipment and contingency procedures should be available for critical systems.

---

## 8. Crowd and Front-of-House Coordination

Front-of-house teams should monitor:

- Queue length
- Ticket scanning
- Security screening
- Seating and standing areas
- Crowd movement
- Exit routes
- Attendee queries
- Accessibility requirements

Where crowd congestion develops, the issue should be escalated to the relevant team leader and Operations Lead.

---

## 9. Communications During the Event

Important communications should be:

- Clear
- Concise
- Consistent
- Timely
- Directed through agreed communication channels

Potential communications include:

- Entry instructions
- Programme announcements
- Safety messages
- Venue information
- Changes to arrangements
- Exit and transport guidance

---

## 10. Event Close

At approximately 23:00:

- Programme concludes
- Event team communicates closing information
- Front-of-house teams support attendee exit
- Security monitors crowd movement
- Transport information is communicated where necessary
- Lost-property procedures remain available
- Venue and supplier teams begin clear-down

The venue should only be considered clear following confirmation from the appropriate operational and venue teams.

---

## 11. Post-Event Handover

Following attendee departure:

1. Complete venue inspection.
2. Record incidents and operational issues.
3. Confirm supplier responsibilities.
4. Begin equipment breakdown where authorised.
5. Remove event materials.
6. Complete venue handover.
7. Record outstanding actions.
8. Begin post-event evaluation.

---

## 12. Operational Communication Chain

A proposed event-day escalation structure is:

**Event Lead**

↓  

**Operations / Production / Security Leads**

↓

**Team Leaders**

↓

**Staff / Volunteers**

Any serious safety, security or operational issue should be escalated immediately rather than handled beyond the individual's level of authority.
