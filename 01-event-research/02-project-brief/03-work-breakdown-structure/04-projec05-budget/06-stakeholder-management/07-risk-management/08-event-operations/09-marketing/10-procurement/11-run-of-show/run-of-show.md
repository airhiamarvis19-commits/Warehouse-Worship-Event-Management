# Event-Day Run of Show – Warehouse Worship: The Gathering 2026

## 1. Purpose

The Run of Show provides an illustrative event-day operational framework for coordinating activities before, during and after a large-scale worship event.

It demonstrates how venue operations, production, security, staffing, programme coordination and attendee management could be sequenced throughout event day.

This is an **illustrative portfolio document** and does not claim to reproduce Warehouse Worship's actual internal event-day running order.

## 24. Key Operational Observation

The main attendee-experience observation used in this case study is **entry congestion**.

The user arrived at approximately 5:00 PM and experienced a significant queue before entering the venue.

The observed entry process included:

**Queue → Security → Ticket Scan → Wristband → Venue Entry**

This demonstrates why arrival planning, queue capacity, security throughput and attendee communication should be considered together within the operational plan.

---

## 25. Operational Improvement Opportunities

Based on the case-study observations, potential improvement opportunities include:

### Improve Entry Throughput

Review the relationship between expected arrival volumes, ticket scanning capacity and security screening capacity.

### Improve Queue Communication

Provide clear pre-event information about arrival times and expected entry arrangements.

### Maintain Clear Wayfinding

Use visible signage at key decision points.

### Monitor Crowd Flow

Use trained staff to identify congestion before it becomes a significant operational issue.

### Integrate Transport Information

Provide attendees with clear information about public transport and other departure options.
