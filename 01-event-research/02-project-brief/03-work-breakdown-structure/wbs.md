# 03 — Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)

## 1. Purpose

The Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) decomposes the event project into manageable work packages and activities.

The WBS provides the foundation for:

- Project scheduling
- Resource planning
- Budget allocation
- Responsibility assignment
- Risk management
- Event-day delivery

# 3. WBS Summary

The project is divided into eleven major work packages:

1. Project Management & Planning
2. Venue & Site Management
3. Programme & Artist Management
4. Production & Technical
5. Marketing & Communications
6. Ticketing & Attendee Management
7. Staffing & Volunteers
8. Security, Safety & Risk
9. Transport & Logistics
10. Event-Day Operations
11. Post-Event Evaluation

This structure provides the foundation for the project's schedule, budget, stakeholder responsibilities and risk management activities.

---

## 4. Project Management Application

The WBS demonstrates the decomposition of a complex event into manageable areas of work.

Each work package can subsequently be assigned:

- An owner
- A start date
- An end date
- Required resources
- Dependencies
- Budget allocation
- Associated risks

This allows the project manager to move from high-level planning to detailed project control.
